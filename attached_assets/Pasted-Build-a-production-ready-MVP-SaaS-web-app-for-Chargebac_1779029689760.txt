Build a production-ready MVP SaaS web app for “ChargebackPilot.de”.

Goal:
ChargebackPilot is an AI-guided chargeback and payment dispute assistant for German consumers. It helps users structure their case, understand which evidence may be useful, choose the right dispute category, and generate professional text templates for PayPal, credit card issuers, Amex, Visa/Mastercard banks, Klarna, and merchants.

IMPORTANT LEGAL POSITIONING:
This is NOT legal advice, NOT a law firm, NOT claims enforcement, and NOT a guarantee of success. It is an AI-powered information, organization, and wording assistant. Add clear disclaimers throughout the product:
“Keine Rechtsberatung. Keine Erfolgsgarantie. ChargebackPilot bietet allgemeine Informationen und KI-gestützte Formulierungshilfe.”
Do not use wording like “we win your case” or “guaranteed refund”.
